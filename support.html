Hinterland

A little help along the way.

Add a book

Tap Add, then choose files or a folder. MP3, M4A and M4B audio are supported. Each import becomes one book. Review the title and drag files into the right order before importing. Protected audio cannot be imported.

Try importing and seeking

Download 90-second test audio (M4B). This file contains artificial tones in three chapters for checking import and seeking; it is not an audiobook.

Continue listening

Opening a book does not start playback. Tap Play when you are ready. The app saves while playing and immediately when you pause or seek. At the end of a book, choose Restart to listen again.

Listen offline

An imported or deliberately downloaded book can play offline. For a cloud-only book, choose Download while connected. An entry appearing in your library does not necessarily mean its audio has finished uploading from another device.

iCloud is waiting

Check that iCloud Drive is enabled, both devices use the same Apple account, and the account has enough storage. Leave the importing device connected while large files upload. Open Settings and choose Sync Now or Retry Sync. Local listening continues even if sync is unavailable.

Google Drive is waiting (Android)

Check that both Android devices use the same Google account, that the account has enough Google Drive storage, and that the device is connected. Leave the importing device connected while large files upload. Open Settings and choose Sync Now or Retry Sync. Local listening continues even if sync is unavailable. The Android app syncs with other Android devices through Google Drive; it does not sync with the iPhone, iPad or Mac app, which use iCloud.

Two saved positions

If two devices listened independently, Hinterland keeps both positions and asks where to continue. Choose the device and position you want. A deliberate rewind is a valid listening position.

Free up space safely

Remove Download is available only after another complete copy is confirmed in iCloud, or in Google Drive on Android. Delete Book is different: it removes the book from your synced library. Disabling sync does not delete cloud data.

Web player help

Which files work? MP3, M4A and M4B files without DRM. Choose several files or a whole folder and they become one book. You can also drag and drop.

A file will not play. It may be protected (DRM), such as Audible AAX, or in a format your browser cannot decode. Try another browser.

Where are my books stored? Only in this browser, on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Browsers can clear stored data, so keep your original files.

How do I move to another browser or device? Add the files again there.

Does it sync with the iPhone app? No. The web player does not sync with the iPhone app or iCloud. The iPhone and iPad app is coming to the App Store.

Contact support

Keep the app installed if you have local-only books, and preserve the original audio files. For help from THREEFOLD LTD, open a support issue on GitHub. A GitHub account is required to create an issue.

Issues are public. Do not upload copyrighted or private audio, or share private account information, personal details, or sensitive screenshots. Describe the problem without them; useful details include your device model, iOS or Android version, app version, what you attempted, and any error message after removing private information.
