Hinterland

Your books. Your space.

Hinterland is an audiobook player operated by THREEFOLD LTD for files you choose to import. It does not require a Hinterland account.

What stays on your device

Your imported audio, embedded artwork, book titles, listening positions and playback preferences are stored in the app’s private storage. Hinterland does not include advertising, analytics or remote error-reporting services. Local diagnostic logs may be produced by Apple’s operating system.

Optional iCloud

Sync is off until you enable it. If enabled, audio files, library information and listening positions are stored in your own Apple iCloud account using iCloud Drive and CloudKit. This uses your iCloud storage and is governed by Apple’s privacy policy and service terms. THREEFOLD LTD does not operate a server that receives your library.

Your choices

Turning sync off stops new app-scheduled cloud activity. Transfers already submitted to Apple may finish. It does not erase the cloud library. Remove Download removes only the recoverable audio on that device. Delete Book removes the app’s library entry and managed audio, including from synced devices when the deletion reaches iCloud. Your original files outside Hinterland are not changed.

Backups and deletion

Local-only imports use ordinary device backup behaviour. Confirmed iCloud-recoverable audio copies are excluded from device backups to avoid duplicating storage. Deleting the app can remove locally stored books. Export or retain your original files before deleting it.

Children and permissions

Hinterland does not request access to contacts, location, the microphone or your photo library. The system file picker gives access only to the files or folders you select.

Questions and support

For help or privacy questions, see the support page or open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public and subject to GitHub’s policies; do not post personal information, copyrighted audio or private audio.
