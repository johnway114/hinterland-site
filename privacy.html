Hinterland

Your books. Your space.

Hinterland is an audiobook player operated by THREEFOLD LTD for files you choose to import. It does not require a Hinterland account.

What stays on your device

Your imported audio, embedded artwork, book titles, listening positions and playback preferences are stored in the app’s private storage. Hinterland does not include advertising, analytics or remote error-reporting services. Local diagnostic logs may be produced by the device’s operating system.

Optional iCloud

Sync is off until you enable it. If enabled, audio files, library information and listening positions are stored in your own Apple iCloud account using iCloud Drive and CloudKit. This uses your iCloud storage and is governed by Apple’s privacy policy and service terms. THREEFOLD LTD does not operate a server that receives your library.

Optional Google Drive (Android)

On Android, sync is also off until you enable it. When you enable it, Google asks you to let Hinterland use its own app data folder in your Google Drive. Hinterland cannot see your other Drive files. Audio files, library information and listening positions are then stored in that private folder in your own Google account. This uses your Google storage and is governed by Google’s privacy policy and service terms. THREEFOLD LTD does not operate a server that receives your library and cannot read it. To remove the stored copy, delete the book in Hinterland, or open Google Drive on the web, choose Settings, then Manage apps, and delete Hinterland’s hidden app data. You can withdraw Hinterland’s access at any time in your Google Account under Third-party apps and services.

Your choices

Turning sync off stops new app-scheduled cloud activity. Transfers already submitted to Apple may finish. It does not erase the cloud library. Remove Download removes only the recoverable audio on that device. Delete Book removes the app’s library entry and managed audio, including from synced devices when the deletion reaches iCloud. Your original files outside Hinterland are not changed.

Backups and deletion

On iPhone, iPad and Mac, local-only imports use ordinary device backup behaviour, and confirmed iCloud-recoverable audio copies are excluded from device backups to avoid duplicating storage. On Android, the library is not included in Google’s cloud backup because audiobooks are too large for it; a direct device-to-device transfer copies it. Deleting the app can remove locally stored books. Export or retain your original files before deleting it.

Children and permissions

Hinterland does not request access to contacts, location, the microphone or your photo library. The system file picker gives access only to the files or folders you select.

The web player

Books you add to the web player are stored only in your browser, on your device. Nothing is uploaded to Hinterland or to anyone else. The web player uses no cookies, no analytics and no ads. The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives ordinary web requests, like any website host. Removing a book, or clearing the site’s data in your browser, deletes the stored copy. The donation link opens Buy Me a Coffee, a separate service with its own privacy policy.

Questions and support

For help or privacy questions, see the support page or open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public and subject to GitHub’s policies; do not post personal information, copyrighted audio or private audio.
