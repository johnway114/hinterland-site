Free audiobook player for MP3, M4A and M4B files

Hinterland plays the audiobooks you already own, right here in your browser. Add a file or a folder, press play, and come back to the same spot later. Nothing is uploaded.

How it works

  1. Add your audiobook

    Choose files or a whole folder, or drag them onto this page. Several files become one book.

  2. Check the order

    Give the book a title. Use the arrows to put the tracks in the right order.

  3. Press play

    Hinterland remembers where you stopped, even after you close the page.

What it does

  • Keeps your place

    Your listening position is saved in this browser as you listen.

  • Shows chapters

    M4B chapters appear in a list. Tap one to jump straight to it.

  • Changes speed

    Listen at 0.75×, 1×, 1.25×, 1.5×, 1.75× or 2×.

  • Sleep timer

    Stop playback after 15, 30 or 60 minutes.

  • Quick skips

    Go back 15 seconds or forward 30 seconds with one tap.

  • One book from many files

    Choose several files or a folder. They play as one book, track after track.

  • Drag and drop

    Drop audio files or a folder anywhere on this page to add a book.

Supported formats

M4B
Audiobook files, with their chapters.
M4A
AAC audio files.
MP3
The most common audio format.

Files must be free of DRM. Audible AAX files and other protected files do not work. If a file will not play, try another browser.

How to play M4B files

Private by design

Your audiobooks are stored only in this browser, in its own storage on your device. They are never uploaded. There is no account, no ads and no tracking.

Browsers can clear stored books, for example when you clear site data or your device runs low on space. Keep your original files so you can add a book again. Read the privacy policy.

Try it with a free test file

No audiobook to hand? Download a 90-second test file in M4B format. It holds three chapters of artificial tones, so you can try chapters, skipping and speed.

Hinterland for iPhone and iPad

A Hinterland app for iPhone and iPad is coming to the App Store. It plays the same MP3, M4A and M4B files. If you turn on sync, it keeps your books and listening position in your own iCloud account.

The web player does not sync with the app or with iCloud. Books you add here stay in this browser.

Questions and answers

Is Hinterland free?

Yes. The web player is free. There is no account, no ads and no tracking.

Are my audiobooks uploaded anywhere?

No. Your files are stored only in this browser, on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Which audiobook formats can I play?

MP3, M4A and M4B files without DRM. You can add one file, several files or a whole folder, and they become one book.

Can I play Audible books?

No. Audible AAX files and other protected (DRM) files do not work in Hinterland.

Does it remember where I stopped?

Yes. Hinterland saves your listening position in this browser. Close the page, come back later and carry on from the same spot.

Does it show M4B chapters?

Yes. In the player, open Chapters & tracks to see the chapters in an M4B file and jump to any of them.

Will my books stay in the browser forever?

Not always. Browsers can clear stored books, for example when you clear site data or your device runs low on space. Keep your original files so you can add them again.

Does the web player sync with the iPhone app?

No. The web player does not sync with the iPhone app or iCloud. The iPhone and iPad app is coming to the App Store.
